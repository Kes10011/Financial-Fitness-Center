Stock Market Investing 101

A clear, structured introduction to how the stock market works and how thoughtful investors analyse companies, read charts, and build diversified portfolios. You will learn the language of the market, how to evaluate a business before you buy, and how to think in years rather than days — with examples relevant to both the Trinidad and Tobago Stock Exchange and global markets. This course is educational, not financial advice, and no returns are promised or guaranteed.

Who it’s for: Ideal for students who already budget and save consistently and now want to understand shares, portfolios, and long-term investing properly before putting money at risk.
