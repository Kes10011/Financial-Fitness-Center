Complete Financial Literacy

This is the foundation course for anyone who wants to feel calm, clear, and in control of their money. You will build a complete personal finance system step by step — from understanding your numbers and budgeting in TT dollars, to managing debt, handling NIS and everyday expenses, and taking your first informed steps into investing. It is practical, jargon-free education designed for real life in Trinidad and Tobago. This course is educational, not financial advice.

Who it’s for: Ideal for beginners, young professionals, and families who earn an income but have never been taught how to organise, protect, and grow it with a clear system.
