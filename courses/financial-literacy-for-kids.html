Financial Literacy for Kids

A story-powered adventure that teaches children how money really works — by travelling through its whole history. Young heroes journey from the barter days of swapping milk and hammers, through kings and gold coins, paper banknotes and bank vaults, all the way to cards, phones, and money today. Then they build the habits that matter: setting savings goals, telling needs from wants, understanding how banks keep money safe, and discovering how money can grow — and be shared. Every lesson is a bright, comic-style story with a friendly guide, big simple ideas, and a “hero mission” children complete off-screen with a parent.

Who it’s for: Parents, grandparents, and godparents who want children (suggested ages 7–12) to grow up money-smart and confident. Bought by adults, loved by kids — no experience needed, just curiosity.
